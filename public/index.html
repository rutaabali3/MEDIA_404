  • Public content only. Posts and product pages everyone can see. Content hidden behind a login can't be reached by any web tool.

  • YouTube: covers and page media only — full video downloads break YouTube's terms.
  • Live streams (m3u8) are listed as links — feed them to yt-dlp / ffmpeg to merge.
